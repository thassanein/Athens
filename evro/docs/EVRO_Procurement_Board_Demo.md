## The walkthrough (six clicks)

1. **Executive Dashboard** — pipeline funnel across all 11 lifecycle stages, forecast curve
   (realized run-rate → risk-adjusted projection), decision queue, at-risk value, savings by
   type, top blockers. *Point out: the 11 stages sum to the $12.3M headline — it reconciles.*

2. **Savings Pipeline** (click a funnel stage) — the whole book grouped by stage, filter by
   savings type. *Point out the eight shared savings definitions — one language for
   Procurement, Finance, Operations and Leadership.*

3. **Opportunity Workspace** (click any opportunity) — the full story on one screen:
   mission header, 11-stage lifecycle, board-ready summary, business case, financial &
   operational impact, supplier & category, dependencies & risks, decision trail, and an AI
   recommendation. *Click **View evidence** — every number drills to its source record.*

4. **Decision Center** — 23 decisions ranked by value, **$2.3M of expected value at stake**.
   Pick the top one: next decision, owner, approvers, due date, confidence, expected value,
   missing evidence, **alternatives**, decision lineage, and *why* the recommendation was
   made. *Approve / return / comment right here — governed by real role entitlement.*

5. **Enterprise AI** — five decision-intelligence briefs (pipeline, risk, forecast variance,
   approvals, realization). *This is intelligence, not a chatbot: every brief carries
   confidence, evidence, assumptions, risks, dependencies and expected value.*

6. **Settings** — the capability model. Procurement is on; Fleet / Operations / CX / HR /
   Finance are declared and off. *Flip one on and the platform reveals the full enterprise
   navigation — same platform, same data model, next capability.*
